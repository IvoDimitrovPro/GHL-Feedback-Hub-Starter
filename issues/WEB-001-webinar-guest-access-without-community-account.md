# WEB-001 — Let webinar guests join without creating a Community account

**Product:** Webinars / Community Events
**Type:** UX
**Status:** New
**Date reported:** 2026-04-29
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-04-29 Product Town Hall
**Impact:** High

## Observed
A public webinar using a Community Personal Meeting Link appears to require attendees to sign in or create a Community account first.

## Why it matters
A webinar funnel should allow a prospect to register and join with minimal friction, even when the event is not intended to acquire a Community member.

## Expected
Let the host accept a registrant’s name and email to grant event access, with an optional choice to create a Community account.

## HighLevel response
No direct response was found in the matching Zoom chat. Current Community Event documentation describes attendee access through signed-in Communities; anonymous guest access through a Personal Meeting Link was not verified.

## Evidence
- Zoom Product Town Hall chat: 2026-04-29.
