# AI-004 — Preserve course content and structure in Ask AI imports

**Product:** Ask AI / Courses
**Type:** Reliability
**Status:** New
**Impact:** Medium
**Date reported:** 2026-07-13
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-07-13 Product Town Hall; 2026-08-20 Product Town Hall

## Observed
Ivo reported Ask AI imports omitting or misreading course content for several clients.

## Why it matters
AI-generated work is unreliable if the source course was imported incompletely.

## Expected
Preserve course content and structure during import and report missing content or import failures clearly.

## HighLevel response
A text-file workflow worked in one follow-up; course/video import behavior was not established as fixed. Capture reproducible examples before escalating as a defect.
