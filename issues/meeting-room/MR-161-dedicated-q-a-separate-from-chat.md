# MR-161 — Dedicated Q&A separate from chat

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** ChatGPT beta feedback conversation; sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md


## Observed
Chat is not a replacement for moderated webinar/class questions.

## Why it matters
Hosts need to triage, assign and answer questions without losing them in chat.

## Expected
Open/answered/dismissed states, upvotes, answer live/private and assign to moderator/panelist.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel agrees dedicated Q&A differs from regular chat and has noted the requirement.
