# MR-140 — Recording Hub as an organized asset library

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High


## Observed
Recordings need to scale beyond a flat meeting list.

## Why it matters
Agencies, recurring meetings, courses and clients quickly create many recordings.

## Expected
Folders/collections, tags, search/filter, rename, move/copy, bulk actions, archive/trash, permissions, ownership and retention.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel agrees recordings should become reusable assets; folders/collections, search, permissions, ownership, and retention are useful.
