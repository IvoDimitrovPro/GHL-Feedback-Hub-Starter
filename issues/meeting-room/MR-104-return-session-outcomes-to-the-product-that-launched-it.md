# MR-104 — Return session outcomes to the product that launched it

**Product:** Courses / Communities / Events
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
Attendance and session outcomes should remain available in their originating Course, Community, or Event.

## Why it matters
Without return-to-source context, attendance and recordings become isolated assets instead of useful product data.

## Expected
Return attendance, recordings, transcripts, replays, and relevant analytics to the Course, Community, Event, or other product that launched the session.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel intends to preserve source product context and return session data to Communities, Courses, Events, and related products.
