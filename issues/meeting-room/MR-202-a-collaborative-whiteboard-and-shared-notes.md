# MR-202 — Add a collaborative whiteboard and shared notes

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)

## Observed
Workshops, coaching, and classroom sessions benefit from collaborative whiteboard and shared-note tools.

## Why it matters
Workshops, coaching, and classroom sessions often need a shared working surface, not only video and chat.

## Expected
Allow participants to work together on shared notes and a whiteboard inside the session.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel described these capabilities as useful and said they can be considered as an additional collaboration capability. Acknowledged; no delivery commitment.
