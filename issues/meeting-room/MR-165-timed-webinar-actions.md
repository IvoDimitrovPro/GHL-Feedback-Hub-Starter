# MR-165 — Timed webinar actions

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High


## Observed
Offers, polls, resources and redirects should be part of the webinar/session timeline.

## Why it matters
This removes dependence on separate webinar software and connects engagement directly to CRM/funnels.

## Expected
Timed/manual CTA, offer, checkout, poll, resource, redirect and workflow actions, tracked per contact.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel agrees timed CTAs, offers, polls, resources, redirects, and workflow triggers are valuable.
