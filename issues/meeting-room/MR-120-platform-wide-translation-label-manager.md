# MR-120 — Platform-wide Translation & Label Manager

**Product:** Platform
**Type:** Platform
**Status:** New
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High


## Observed
Translation/custom-label controls are implemented feature by feature.

## Why it matters
This creates repeated development work and prevents agencies from consistently localizing/renaming client-facing UI.

## Expected
One searchable platform string catalog with Agency → Location → asset overrides, import/export, bulk AI translation, missing-key view and reset-to-default.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel says a platform-wide Translation & Label Manager belongs at platform level; Meeting Room can consume it when available. Still a new platform-level request.
