# MR-007 — Unified recording → transcript → AI post-meeting flow

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High


## Observed
Recording, transcription and AI processing are still separate steps.

## Why it matters
Recurring client/team meetings should produce usable notes and assets automatically.

## Expected
Per-room/session settings for auto-record, auto-transcribe, AI summary/action items, CRM save and optional replay publishing.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel agrees recording, transcription, AI summaries, and post-meeting processing should work as one configurable flow; feedback noted.
