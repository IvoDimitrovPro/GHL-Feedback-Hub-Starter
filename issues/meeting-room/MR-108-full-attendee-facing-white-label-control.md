# MR-108 — Full attendee-facing white-label control

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** ChatGPT beta feedback conversation; sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md


## Observed
Custom domains exist, but attendee-facing room surfaces still need broader branding control.

## Why it matters
Agencies should be able to make the meeting experience feel native to each brand.

## Expected
Agency/location/room overrides for logo, favicon, page title, colors, waiting-room content and other attendee-facing UI.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel noted the feedback and will account for broader white-label customization across attendee-facing Meeting Room experiences.
