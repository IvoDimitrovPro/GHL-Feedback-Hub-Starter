# MR-163 — Play videos/media natively inside the room

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** ChatGPT beta feedback conversation; sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md


## Observed
Showing a video currently relies on screen sharing.

## Why it matters
Native playback can provide better quality, synchronization and reuse of HighLevel Media Storage.

## Expected
Select/upload video from Media Storage, synchronized playback for everyone, host play/pause/seek controls, and correct inclusion in the final recording.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel agrees native slide/video/media presentation could improve quality and reliable audio capture; feedback noted.
