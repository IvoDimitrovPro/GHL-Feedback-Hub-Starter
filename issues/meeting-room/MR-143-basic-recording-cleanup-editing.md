# MR-143 — Basic recording cleanup/editing

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** Medium


## Observed
Raw recordings often need small cleanup before reuse.

## Why it matters
Client delivery, course publishing and webinar replay should not require a separate editor for simple changes.

## Expected
Rename, trim start/end, thumbnail, chapters, transcript/caption edit and export.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel noted trimming, clips, thumbnails, captions/transcript editing, and export controls as useful additions.
