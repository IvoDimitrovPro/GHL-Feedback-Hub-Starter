# MR-210 — API/webhooks for meeting objects and events

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Platform
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High


## Observed
External integrations need programmatic access to Meeting Room data.

## Why it matters
Meeting Room becomes more useful as a platform primitive when other systems can react to it.

## Expected
APIs/webhooks for rooms, occurrences, participants, recordings, transcripts and engagement events.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel agrees rooms, sessions, participants, recordings, transcripts, and engagement events should be exposed through APIs/webhooks.
