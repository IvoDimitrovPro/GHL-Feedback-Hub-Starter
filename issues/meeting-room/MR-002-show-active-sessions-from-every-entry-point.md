# MR-002 — Show active sessions from every entry point

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** UX
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** Medium

## Observed
Sessions started in another HighLevel area should still appear in the central manager.

## Why it matters
A central manager is unreliable if live sessions disappear simply because they were started from another entry point.

## Expected
Show any active Meeting Room session in the central manager regardless of where it was started, with clear live state and enter/manage actions.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel will also account for consistently showing active sessions started through non-Calendar Meeting Room flows.
