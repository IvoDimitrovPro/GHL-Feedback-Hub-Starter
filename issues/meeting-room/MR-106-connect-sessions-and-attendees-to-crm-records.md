# MR-106 — Connect sessions and attendees to CRM records

**Product:** CRM
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
Relevant session data should be usable from related contact/opportunity history.

## Why it matters
CRM-native meeting data is required for useful follow-up and is a key advantage over standalone meeting tools.

## Expected
Store relevant attendance, recording, transcript, AI notes, and engagement data on the related Contact, Appointment, and Opportunity records.

## HighLevel response
Attendance, recordings, transcripts, AI notes, and engagement data are already planned for integration with relevant CRM entities.
