# MR-209 — Connect session analytics to source and conversion reporting

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** High

## Observed
Session analytics should show where attendees came from and what happened after attendance.

## Why it matters
Raw attendance metrics become actionable only when they connect to acquisition and business outcomes.

## Expected
Connect registration and session analytics to acquisition source, attendance/engagement, downstream conversion, and revenue reporting.

## HighLevel response
Deeper session, attendance, engagement, recording, transcript, and later source/conversion/revenue reporting is already a major part of HighLevel plan.
