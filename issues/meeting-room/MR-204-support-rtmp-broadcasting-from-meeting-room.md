# MR-204 — Support RTMP broadcasting from Meeting Room

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** Medium

## Observed
Some events need to reach external streaming destinations.

## Why it matters
Native broadcast output lets an event reach external audiences without adding another meeting/streaming stack.

## Expected
Support RTMP broadcasting from Meeting Room to configured external destinations with clear connection/status feedback.

## HighLevel response
RTMP streaming is already in HighLevel plan for supported external destinations.
