# MR-005 — Create instant or scheduled meetings from the manager

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Planned
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** [Meeting Room beta response](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md)
**Impact:** Medium

## Observed
Users need both common meeting creation paths directly in Meeting Room.

## Why it matters
Common meeting creation should not require leaving the Meeting Room product for another module.

## Expected
Provide Instant Meeting and Schedule Meeting actions directly in the Meeting Room manager, with security/access configurable per meeting.

## HighLevel response
Calendar supports appointment-specific meetings today. Instant Meeting and Schedule Meeting inside Meeting Room are planned; access/security will be configurable per meeting.
