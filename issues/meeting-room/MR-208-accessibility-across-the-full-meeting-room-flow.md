# MR-208 — Accessibility across the full Meeting Room flow

**Product:** Meeting Room
**Type:** UX
**Status:** Acknowledged
**Date reported:** 2026-09-25
**Source:** ChatGPT beta feedback conversation; sources/highlevel-meeting-room-response-2026-09-25.md


## Observed
Accessibility needs to cover join, live room and post-session screens.

## Why it matters
Keyboard/screen-reader/caption usability should not depend on the specific surface.

## Expected
Keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, correct focus order, captions and sufficient contrast.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel agrees keyboard navigation, screen-reader support, focus management, captions, and contrast should be considered across Meeting Room.
