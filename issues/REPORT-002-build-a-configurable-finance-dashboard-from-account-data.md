# REPORT-002 — Build a configurable finance dashboard from account data

**Product:** Reporting / Finance
**Type:** Feature
**Status:** New
**Impact:** High
**Date reported:** 2026-05-08
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-05-08 Product Town Hall

## Observed
Ivo asked for a native view joining contacts, purchases, products, ad spend, and transactions with custom widgets/AI analysis.

## Why it matters
Fragmented finance data slows basic business decisions and makes account-level performance harder to understand.

## Expected
Provide a configurable finance dashboard that can combine contacts, purchases, products, transactions, advertising spend, and other account data with custom widgets and AI analysis.

## HighLevel response
No complete native equivalent verified.
