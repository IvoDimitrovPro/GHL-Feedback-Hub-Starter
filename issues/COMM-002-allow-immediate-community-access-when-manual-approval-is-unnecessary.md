# COMM-002 — Allow immediate Community access when manual approval is unnecessary

**Product:** Communities
**Type:** UX
**Status:** New
**Impact:** Medium
**Date reported:** 2026-05-22
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-05-22 Product Town Hall

## Observed
Ivo asked to let eligible members join without waiting for admin approval.

## Why it matters
Manual approval delays access for people who are already eligible or have already paid.

## Expected
Allow configured Communities/groups/events to grant immediate access to eligible members without manual approval.

## HighLevel response
No implementation or response established.
