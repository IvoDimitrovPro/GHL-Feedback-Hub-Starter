# INVOICE-001 — Customize invoices and use customer-entered VAT/company details

**Product:** Invoices / Documents
**Type:** Customization
**Status:** Planned
**Impact:** High
**Date reported:** 2026-04-14
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-04-14 Product Town Hall

## Observed
The invoice template and customer-entered legal/tax data did not meet Ivo's jurisdictional workflow.

## Why it matters
Correct legal/tax data and document formatting are required for usable billing in the agency's jurisdiction.

## Expected
Allow full invoice/document template customization and map customer-entered company, VAT/tax, and address values into invoices/receipts automatically.

## HighLevel response
HighLevel discussed pixel-level document customization and custom values for receipts; Ivo still reported the gap in July. Delivery unverified.
