# COMM-003 — Support light and dark logos across member-facing products

**Product:** Communities / Courses
**Type:** Branding
**Status:** New
**Impact:** Medium
**Date reported:** 2026-05-22
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-05-22 Product Town Hall

## Observed
A single logo treatment does not work on both light and dark backgrounds across Community, Courses, and Portal.

## Why it matters
A single logo treatment produces visibly broken branding when member-facing surfaces switch between light and dark backgrounds.

## Expected
Support separate light and dark logo variants and use the appropriate asset automatically across member-facing surfaces.

## HighLevel response
No general cross-surface implementation verified.
