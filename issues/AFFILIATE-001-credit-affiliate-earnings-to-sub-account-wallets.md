# AFFILIATE-001 — Credit affiliate earnings to sub-account wallets

**Product:** Affiliate / SaaS Mode
**Type:** Billing
**Status:** New
**Impact:** Medium
**Date reported:** 2026-08-11
**Source:** [Zoom feedback index](https://github.com/IvoDimitrovPro/highlevel-product-feedback/blob/main/sources/zoom-feedback-index-2026.md) — 2026-08-11 Product Town Hall

## Observed
Ivo suggested using earned commission as wallet credit for email/AI usage.

## Why it matters
This removes manual commission payouts/credits when the intended value is immediately reused inside the platform.

## Expected
Allow affiliate earnings to be credited directly to a sub-account wallet and used for HighLevel usage costs such as email or AI, with a clear transaction history.

## HighLevel response
No direct response or implementation established.
