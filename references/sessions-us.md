# Sessions.us — product reference

Sessions.us is kept here as a product/UX reference, not as evidence that HighLevel lacks a specific feature.

## Useful references

- [AppSumo product page](https://appsumo.com/products/sessions/) — feature overview and historical user reviews.
- [Sessions: agenda/tools walkthrough](https://medium.com/sessions-us/the-ultimate-guide-to-a-hyper-optimized-15-min-investor-pitch-8b77b22c89fe) — shows the agenda-driven meeting model and native content steps.
- [Phil Pallen walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1o57i3KirCA) — visual tour of the meeting experience.

## Patterns worth borrowing

- **Agenda as the meeting runtime** — each agenda step can change what participants are working with, rather than acting as a passive notes list.
- **Native content inside the room** — presentations, video, websites/tools, polls, questions, and other resources without constant window juggling.
- **Reusable session templates** — repeat a proven meeting/workshop structure without rebuilding it.
- **Post-meeting memory** — keep recording, transcript, chat, polls, notes, resources, and takeaways together as one session context.
- **Private participant notes + shared takeaways** — useful for client calls, workshops, and internal meetings.

The useful lesson is the workflow model, not copying Sessions feature-for-feature. HighLevel can go further by connecting the same session context to CRM, Workflows, Courses, Communities, Events, Client Portal, and revenue attribution.
